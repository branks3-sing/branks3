About Sing Along

Sing Along is a karaoke-based application designed to provide a smooth singing and recording experience. Users can sing along with music tracks while recording their performance in real time.

This platform allows controlled access, where only authorized users created by the administrator or developer can login. Public registration is not available.

The application is focused on simple karaoke playback and recording functionality without storing or processing any user data.

Key Features:

  • Real-time karaoke recording experience
  • Music tracks for karaoke singing
  • Lyrics display for easy singing
  • Restricted and controlled access
  • No data collection or storage

This platform is developed to enable karaoke singing and recording functionality in a simple and secure environment.

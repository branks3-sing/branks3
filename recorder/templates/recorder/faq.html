Frequently Asked Questions

What is Branks3 Sing Along?

Branks3 Sing Along is an online karaoke singing and recording platform.

Can I record my singing?

Yes. Users can record voice and video directly in the browser.

Does it support mobile devices?

Yes. The platform works on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop devices.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Everything works directly inside the browser.
